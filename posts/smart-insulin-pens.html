Smart Insulin Pens: A Bridge to Better Management

By Beyond Diabetes · Digital Infrastructure Chair

For the majority of people managing insulin-dependent diabetes worldwide, pumps remain inaccessible due to cost, availability, or preference. Smart insulin pens represent a meaningful middle ground — bringing data logging and dosing intelligence to a familiar form factor without the upfront cost or commitment of an insulin pump.

What makes a pen "smart"

The core feature is automatic dose logging: time, units, and insulin type are recorded and transmitted to an app without manual entry. This alone eliminates a common source of management error — forgotten doses or duplicate dosing. The NovoPen 6 accomplishes this passively through Bluetooth; users simply inject and the data appears in the app.

InPen's bolus calculator

The Medtronic InPen goes further with an integrated bolus calculator that factors in carbohydrate intake, current CGM reading (via integration with Dexcom or Libre), insulin-on-board, and individual settings. For users who find manual carb-ratio math stressful or error-prone, this is a substantive benefit. The system is FDA-cleared as a medical device, not just a wellness app.

Insurance coverage for smart pens is improving but inconsistent. The NovoPen 6 is a reusable device covered under durable medical equipment (DME) by some plans; the InPen has broader coverage. Checking your specific plan before choosing a device is worth doing — out-of-pocket costs can vary dramatically.

The data integration story

The real value of smart pens emerges when their dose data is layered against CGM readings. Seeing that your 8pm dose was 3 units while your glucose had already been dropping — or that you forgot a lunchtime injection — produces actionable insight that retrospective A1c measurements can't provide. Both Abbott and Dexcom have built pen-compatible data views into their platforms.
