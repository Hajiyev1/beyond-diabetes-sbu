Dexcom G7 vs. Abbott Libre 3: What the Data Actually Shows

By Beyond Diabetes · Digital Infrastructure Chair

Continuous glucose monitors have become a cornerstone of modern Type 1 management and are increasingly used by those with Type 2 as well. In 2026, the two most widely used consumer CGMs remain the Dexcom G7 and the Abbott FreeStyle Libre 3 — but comparing them requires looking past marketing claims at what independent clinical data actually shows.

Accuracy

Mean Absolute Relative Difference (MARD) is the primary clinical benchmark for CGM accuracy. A lower MARD means readings that better match venous blood glucose. The G7 has published MARD values around 8.2%, while the Libre 3 has reported approximately 7.9% in its pivotal trial — statistically comparable, and both well below the FDA's 10% benchmark for CGM approval.

What MARD doesn't capture: how often a sensor fails during the wear period, lag time in rapidly changing glucose scenarios, and accuracy during hypoglycemia. These are areas where real-world data can differ significantly from controlled trial outcomes.

Wear time & design

The G7 offers a 10-day wear period with a fully disposable transmitter/sensor combo — no separate transmitter to charge or swap. Its form factor is small and the new "grace period" feature allows a replacement sensor to be pre-soaked while the current one is still active. The Libre 3 extends wear to 14 days and is the world's smallest CGM, making it particularly discrete on the arm.

Smartphone integration

Both devices offer real-time readings on iOS and Android. The G7 supports Apple Watch natively without needing a phone present, which is a meaningful advantage for active users. The Libre 3 now offers real-time streaming after earlier versions required a scan — a major improvement for their platform.

Bottom line

For most users, the choice between G7 and Libre 3 will come down to insurance coverage, desired wear time, and whether pump integration is needed. Users pairing with an Omnipod 5 or Tandem t:slim will need the Dexcom; the Libre 3 integrates with its own ecosystem and some Abbott-partnered systems.
