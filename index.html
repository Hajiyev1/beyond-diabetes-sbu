What we do

Built on three pillars

We bring together students, educators, and the broader Stony Brook community to foster an informed and inclusive space around diabetes.

Education
Monthly deep-dives on emerging technology — CGMs, closed-loop systems, insulin delivery, and clinical research — written to be accessible and honest.
Community
A safe, inclusive space for students living with diabetes, those supporting someone who does, or anyone wanting to learn more about the condition.
Advocacy
Working toward better campus support for students managing chronic conditions — from dining accommodations to accessible healthcare resources.
