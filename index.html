Our mission

A supportive, inclusive, and educational community

Beyond Diabetes creates a space for students affected by diabetes, as well as for those who want to understand and support individuals living with the condition.

Education
Monthly research on diabetes technology, from glucose monitors to insulin delivery, written to be clear, accurate, and accessible.
Advocacy
Working toward better support for students managing diabetes on campus, from dining accommodations to accessible healthcare resources.
Awareness
Sharing accurate information about diabetes to reduce stigma and replace common misconceptions with understanding.
Peer Connection
A place for students to meet others who understand, share experiences, and support one another.
Who we are for

Everyone is welcome

Whether diabetes is part of your life, affects someone you care about, or you simply want to learn and make a difference, there is a place for you here.

Diabetes is part of your life
Connect with peers who understand daily life with diabetes, and find resources that fit student life.
Someone you care about is affected
Learn how to support a friend, roommate, partner, or family member who lives with diabetes.
You want to learn and make a difference
Understand diabetes, challenge common myths, and help build a more informed campus.

Join us in building a more informed, connected, and compassionate campus beyond the diagnosis.
