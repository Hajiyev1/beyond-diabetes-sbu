About Beyond Diabetes.

Mission

Who we are

We are a student-led organization creating a welcoming community for everyone affected by diabetes. Through education, advocacy, awareness, and peer connection, we aim to reduce stigma and remind students that no one has to navigate diabetes alone.

Whether diabetes is part of your life, affects someone you care about, or you simply want to learn and make a difference, join us in building a more informed, connected, and compassionate campus beyond the diagnosis.

Technology is reshaping diabetes care, from continuous glucose monitors that replace fingersticks with real-time readings to insulin pumps that adjust dosing automatically. Beyond Diabetes keeps our community informed about these advances. Our Digital Infrastructure Chair runs this website and publishes monthly research that explains new devices and emerging science in plain, honest language. We want every member, whether they live with diabetes, support someone who does, or are simply curious, to understand the tools that could shape their health and feel confident discussing them with their care team.
